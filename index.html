Flare: Duck Intel

Privacy policy. Effective November 17, 2026.

Flare is a private duck hunting planner for one hunting group, run by Peter Huggins ("we"). Only people the administrator has approved can sign in. This page explains what the app collects, why, and who can see it.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Your email address and sign-in password (the password is stored only in scrambled form by our sign-in provider)To let you sign in. The administrator creates your account.
Your display nameTo show who logged a shared hunt.
Hunt log entries you create: date, start and end time, blind, field, spread, who hunted with you, ducks taken by species, notes, and the app's rating for that dayTo keep the group's shared hunt log, show past hunts and "On this day", and let you export your data.
A saved copy on your phone of your sign-in session, the last data files and your huntsSo the app opens and works with no signal.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your phone's location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. The weather and duck maps use one fixed, approximate area, not your location.

Who can see your information

  • The other members of the hunting group can see the shared hunt log, including who logged each hunt and the party names on it. The administrator can also see and change any hunt.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (sign-in, database and file storage) and GitHub (stores the app's code and runs scheduled jobs, including a weekly backup of the hunt log). They process data only to provide those services.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather forecast. Your phone asks it for the forecast for a fixed area, so it can see your phone's IP address like any website you visit, but it receives no account details.
  • An AI service reads public wildlife-agency web pages for us. No personal information is sent to it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Hunt entries are kept until they are deleted. You can delete your own hunts in the app; a deleted hunt disappears from the app but remains in weekly backups, which keep the newest 12 weeks, after which it is gone. If you leave the group, ask us to remove your account and, if you want, your hunts.

Your choices

  • Export your hunts to Excel in the app at any time.
  • Delete your own hunts in the app.
  • Ask us to delete your account and the hunts you logged, or to correct your name or email, by writing to phuggins17@msn.com.

Security

All traffic uses encrypted connections, and the database allows only approved members to read or add hunts, and only you (or the administrator) to change yours.

Children

Flare is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
